# A saved inventory

Use the reference supplied by the facilitator. Record each answer and the
heading or passage you used to find it. Work from the supplied materials.

It is **14 May 2030 UTC**. Your team has a local inventory file observed on
**3 May 2030 UTC** and copied to your workstation today. The team uses the
default age limit. You have permission to inspect the saved file offline.

1. Which date belongs in `--observed-at`? What status should you expect when
   importing that file today?
2. A colleague also has a fresh report with `status: discovered` and one
   catalog row. What access and execution status can the incident log
   attribute to that observation?
3. Can the team use this row alone to promise a working model handoff?
   Give the reason for your decision.

Submit your answers, or mark a question unanswered if you cannot find the
information in the assigned document.
